# Real anonymous questionnaire

Use this only if you decide to replace the synthetic pilot with actual anonymous responses.

## Intro text

This anonymous questionnaire studies how students' financial capacity, previous social exposure and regional context relate to participation and belonging in college social life. Do not enter your name, ID, phone number or any personally identifiable information.

11. **How strongly do you feel that you belong within the campus community?**

12. **Roughly how many people would you count as regular social contacts on campus?**

13. **For each activity below, how often did you participate during the last month?**

    Rows:

## Important analysis note

Do not ask respondents to name friends. The network used in this project is a student-to-activity participation network, so the questionnaire remains anonymous.
